Chapter 1 · Lectures L1 & L2

Wave & Optics 波動與光學

Harmonic Oscillation & Vibrations 簡諧振盪與振動

An oscillation is a regular periodic motion in time (e.g., mass-spring on a frictionless surface, simple pendulum). A vibration is movement about a mean position (linear, circular, periodic, or non-periodic) — some are useful (loudspeaker membrane), while most are unwanted (machine shaking).

振盪是隨時間規律重複的週期運動（如無摩擦彈簧振子、單擺）。振動是物體繞平衡位置的運動，有些是有用的（喇叭振膜），但多數是有害的（機器震動）。

$$y = A\sin(\omega t), \quad \omega = \frac{2\pi}{T}, \quad f = \frac{1}{T}, \quad v = \frac{\lambda}{T} = f\lambda$$

Amplitude ($A$): max displacement from equilibrium (vertical distance, NOT peak-to-peak). Wavelength ($\lambda$): crest-to-crest distance. For a given medium (fixed $v$), $f$ is inversely proportional to $\lambda$. 振幅 $A$ 是由平衡位置到波峰的垂直距離（非波峰到波谷）。在同一介質中波速 $v$ 固定，頻率 $f$ 與波長 $\lambda$ 成反比。

What is a Wave? & Wave Types 波的本質與類型

A wave is a periodic motion in both space and time that transports energy and information over long distances without transporting matter (particles only oscillate locally around equilibrium).

波是在空間和時間上皆具週期性的運動，能長距離傳遞能量與資訊，但不傳遞物質（介質粒子只在原位附近來回振動）。

  • Transverse Wave (橫波): Particles vibrate perpendicular ($\perp$) to propagation; consists of crests & troughs. Examples: light waves, water waves, waves on a string. 質點振動方向垂直於傳播方向，由波峰與波谷組成（如光波、水波）。
  • Longitudinal Wave (縱波): Particles vibrate parallel ($\parallel$) to propagation; consists of compressions (high density/pressure) and rarefactions (low density/pressure). Example: sound waves in air. Often converted to a transverse sine graph for clearer visualization. 質點振動方向平行於傳播方向，由密部與疏部組成（如聲波）；常轉畫成橫波圖以便觀察。
  • Phase Difference (相位差): Points $180^\circ$ out of phase are separated by $\lambda/2$ (opposite displacement and velocity). 相位差 $180^\circ$ 的兩點相距半個波長 $\lambda/2$。

Mechanical Nature: Sound is a longitudinal mechanical wave requiring a material medium (solid, liquid, or gas). It cannot travel in vacuum (unlike light). Sound travels faster in denser/stiffer media (faster in water/solids than in air; $v_{\text{air}} \approx 340\text{ m/s}$).

聲波是縱向機械波，必須依靠物質介質傳播，不能在真空中傳播；水中聲速快於空氣（空氣中約 $340\text{ m/s}$）。

⚡ Key Real-World Example: Lightning & Thunder

Light arrives almost instantly. If thunder is heard 3 s later, distance $d = 340\text{ m/s} \times 3\text{ s} = 1020\text{ m} \approx 1\text{ km}$. 閃電後 3 秒聽到雷聲，代表雷電距離約 1000 米。

  • Pitch (音調) $\to$ Frequency ($f$). Higher $f$ = higher pitch.
  • Loudness (響度) $\to$ Amplitude ($A$). Intensity $I \propto A^2$ (units: $\text{W/m}^2$).
  • Timbre / Quality (音色) $\to$ Waveform. Fourier Analysis shows any periodic musical sound is a sum of simple sine waves of different frequencies and amplitudes. 傅立葉分析：任何週期性樂音皆可分解為多個不同頻率與振幅的正弦波疊加。
  • Noise vs Music: Noise = irregular vibration; White noise = mixture of many frequencies; Music = periodic tones. 噪音為不規則振動；白噪音包含各種頻率混合；音樂為週期性音調。

Hearing Range: Young humans hear 20 Hz – 20,000 Hz. Below 20 Hz = Infrasound (elephants: 14–12,000 Hz). Above 20,000 Hz = Ultrasound (cats 48–75k, dogs 64–45k, bats 7,000–200,000 Hz; dolphins use echolocation; humans use ultrasound for prenatal medical imaging & submarine sonar).

人耳聽覺範圍為 20–20,000 Hz。低於 20 Hz 為次聲波（大象可聽）；高於 20,000 Hz 為超聲波（蝙蝠、海豚回聲定位、醫學超聲波掃描、聲納）。

$$\text{Sound Level (dB)} = 10 \log_{10}\!\left(\frac{I}{I_0}\right), \quad I_0 = 10^{-12}\text{ W/m}^2 \text{ (0 dB)}$$

Threshold of hearing: $10^{-12}\text{ W/m}^2$ (0 dB); Whisper: 20 dB; Home conversation: 60 dB; Busy street: 70 dB; Threshold of pain / siren: $1\text{ W/m}^2$ (120 dB); Jet 30 m away: 140 dB.
Reflection & Absorption: Concert halls suspend reflectors above stage to direct sound; anechoic quiet rooms use spongy walls to absorb sound (converted to heat). 音樂廳天花設反射板導引聲音；消音室用海綿狀吸音牆把聲能轉為熱能。

Interference & Noise Cancellation 干涉與主動降噪

Constructive interference (in-phase) reinforces amplitude; destructive interference ($180^\circ$ out-of-phase) cancels waves. Noise-canceling earphones record ambient noise with a mic and emit an equal-amplitude wave phase-shifted by $180^\circ$ (anti-noise) to cancel it.

相長干涉使振幅增強，相消干涉（相位差 $180^\circ$）使波抵銷。主動降噪耳機透過麥克風收集噪音並發出反相 $180^\circ$ 聲波來抵銷環境噪音。

Standing Waves 駐波

Formed by two identical waves traveling in opposite directions. Nodes (N) have zero amplitude; Antinodes (A) have maximum amplitude. For a string or open-open pipe of length $L$: $\lambda_n = \frac{2L}{n}$ ($n=1,2,3\dots$). String wave speed: $v = \sqrt{\tau/\rho}$ (higher tension $\tau \Rightarrow$ faster wave & higher pitch; higher linear density $\rho \Rightarrow$ slower wave & lower pitch). Applications: guitar frets shorten $L$ for higher pitch; organ pipes; acoustic levitation traps tiny particles in pressure standing waves.

兩列相同但反向傳播的波疊加形成駐波。波節振幅為零，波腹振幅最大。弦線張力越大波速越快、密度越大波速越慢。應用包括吉他按品縮短弦長提高音調、管風琴及超聲波聲懸浮。

Beats 拍頻

Two waves with slightly different frequencies superpose to produce periodic amplitude modulation: $f_{\text{beat}} = |f_1 - f_2|$. Used in piano tuning (adjust string until beats with a 256 Hz tuning fork disappear; 2 beats/s means string is 254 or 258 Hz).

兩列頻率相近的波疊加會產生振幅週期性強弱變化（拍頻 $f_{\text{beat}} = |f_1 - f_2|$），鋼琴調音師藉此調準琴弦。

Doppler Effect, Bow Waves & Shock Waves 都卜勒效應、船首波與衝擊波

Relative motion between source and observer shifts the observed frequency (approaching $\Rightarrow$ higher $f'$; receding $\Rightarrow$ lower $f'$):

$$\text{Approaching: } f' = \frac{v + v_{\text{obs}}}{v - v_{\text{src}}} f \qquad \text{Receding: } f' = \frac{v - v_{\text{obs}}}{v + v_{\text{src}}} f$$

Bow waves occur when $v_{\text{src}} > v_{\text{wave}}$ (V-shaped constructive interference cone on water). A supersonic aircraft creates a 3D shock wave (high-pressure cone at bow + low-pressure cone at tail $\Rightarrow$ N-shaped pressure wave / sonic boom).

當波源速率超過波速時，波前重疊形成 V 形船首波；超音速飛機則產生三維衝擊波（機頭高壓錐、機尾低壓錐，地面氣壓呈 N 字形，即音爆）。

Light, Vision & Colour 光的本質、視覺與色彩

Light is a transverse electromagnetic (EM) wave generated by accelerating/vibrating electric charges (electrons). Needs no medium; $c = 3\times 10^8\text{ m/s}$ in vacuum. Visible spectrum: 380–750 nm (~400–790 THz).

光是由加速振動的電子產生的橫向電磁波，無需介質即可在真空中以 $3\times 10^8\text{ m/s}$ 傳播。可見光波長約 380–750 nm。

  • Retina Sensors: Rods handle low-light vision; Cones (L-red, M-green, S-blue) handle colour & detail. Eye is most sensitive to yellowish-green (stimulates M & L equally). 視桿細胞負責暗視覺；視錐細胞（紅綠藍）負責彩色，人眼對黃綠光最敏感。
  • RGB Additive vs CMYK Subtractive: Screens & Bayer camera filters emit/detect RGB ("false white" = R+G+B; Pointillism uses eye-mixing). Printing uses CMYK inks that absorb light: Cyan absorbs Red, Magenta absorbs Green, Yellow absorbs Blue, Black (K) absorbs all. 螢幕用 RGB 加色法（點彩畫派原理）；印刷用 CMYK 減色法（青吸紅、品紅吸綠、黃吸藍、黑全吸）。
  • Photography History: Pinhole camera (smaller hole = sharper but dimmer) $\to$ lens camera (sharp & bright). Louis Daguerre (1837 daguerreotype using silver compounds), George Eastman (1884 flexible film, Kodak 1889).

Ray vs Wave Optics & Reflection 幾何光學、波動光學與反射

Ray Optics ($\lambda \ll d$): reflection, refraction, imaging. Wave Optics ($\lambda \approx d$): diffraction, interference, polarization. Law of Reflection: $\theta_I = \theta_R$ (holds for specular mirrors AND rough diffuse surfaces). Parabolic mirrors focus parallel rays cleanly; large spherical mirrors suffer from spherical aberration. Stealth aircraft angle flat surfaces to deflect radar away from the receiver.

幾何光學（$\lambda \ll d$）處理反射、折射、成像；波動光學（$\lambda \approx d$）處理繞射、干涉、偏振。反射定律 $\theta_I=\theta_R$ 適用於所有表面；隱形戰機利用特定傾角反射雷達波以降低雷達截面積。

Refraction, Mirages & Total Internal Reflection 折射、海市蜃樓與全內反射

$$n = \frac{c}{v}, \qquad n_1 \sin\theta_1 = n_2 \sin\theta_2, \qquad \sin\theta_c = \frac{n_2}{n_1} \; (n_1 > n_2)$$

Refractive indices: Air $\approx 1.0003$, Water $\approx 1.33$, Glass $\approx 1.5$, Diamond $\approx 2.417$ ($v = 1.24\times 10^8\text{ m/s}$). When light enters a denser medium (air $\to$ water), speed $v$ decreases, wavelength $\lambda$ decreases, frequency $f$ stays constant. Submerged objects appear shallower (apparent depth; bent pencil).

光從空氣進入水中，波速與波長變小，但頻率不變。折射使水中物體看起來更淺。

  • Mirages (海市蜃樓): $n_{\text{cool air}} > n_{\text{hot air}}$. Inferior mirage (hot road / desert / sunset) bends sky light upward. Superior mirage (warm air over cold sea) bends light downward creating a "flying ship".
  • Total Internal Reflection (TIR): Occurs when light travels from $n_1 > n_2$ at $\theta_1 > \theta_c$. Basis of optical fibres (invented in 1966 by Sir Charles Kuen Kao 高錕教授).
  • Dispersion & Rainbow: $n$ depends on frequency (violet bends more than red). Raindrops refract, internally reflect, and disperse sunlight at $\sim 42^\circ$.
  • Rayleigh Scattering: Atmospheric molecules scatter shorter wavelengths (violet/blue) much more strongly $\Rightarrow$ blue sky. At sunset, light passes through a thicker slice of air, leaving less-scattered red/orange light to reach our eyes.

Thin Lens Equation & Magnification: Convex (converging, $f>0$) vs Concave (diverging, $f<0$). Any 2 of the 3 principal rays locate the image: (1) Parallel $\to$ through $F_2$; (2) Through $F_1 \to$ parallel; (3) Through lens center $\to$ undeflected.

$$\frac{1}{f} = \frac{1}{i} + \frac{1}{p}, \qquad M = \frac{\text{Image size}}{\text{Object size}} = -\frac{i}{p}$$
Object ($p$)Image ($i$)Nature 成像性質
$p > 2f$$f < i < 2f$Real, inverted, diminished 實像、倒立、縮小
$p = 2f$$i = 2f$Real, inverted, same size 實像、倒立、等大
$f < p < 2f$$i > 2f$Real, inverted, magnified 實像、倒立、放大
$p = f$$\infty$Parallel rays (no image) 平行光、不成像

  • Rochester Cloak: Uses a 4-lens optical system to bend light around an object, creating cloaking windows of invisibility. 羅徹斯特隱形斗篷利用四片透鏡組使光線繞過物體實現隱形。
  • Antireflective (AR) Coating: Thin film of thickness $\lambda/4$ causes reflected rays from top and bottom interfaces to interfere destructively and cancel out. 減反射鍍膜厚度為 $\lambda/4$，使上下表面反射光相消干涉。
  • Human Eye: Cornea does most focusing; crystalline lens is variable (controlled by ciliary muscle) to focus on objects at different distances; iris = aperture; retina = image sensor.
  • Single Lens Limitations: Very short $f$ (~2.5 mm for 100x) is hard to manufacture; suffers from spherical aberration (edge rays focus closer) and chromatic aberration (blue & red focus at different points) $\Rightarrow$ solved by compound lenses.
  • Telescope vs Microscope: Telescope prioritizes light-collecting power (large objective diameter) & diffraction-limited resolution (affected by air turbulence $\to$ Hubble in space). Microscope targets high magnification with image at 25 cm near point.
  • 3D Movies & Polarization: Early 3D used red/cyan colour filters. Modern 3D (RealD) uses linear or circular polarizers to separate left-eye and right-eye images. Polarized sunglasses block horizontal road glare.

Blackbody & Infrared (IR) 黑體輻射與紅外線

All objects $>0\text{ K}$ emit EM radiation (Planck's Law). Body ($37^\circ\text{C}$) $\to$ IR; Red hot ($3000\text{ K}$, tungsten bulb <5% visible efficiency); White hot ($6000\text{ K}$, Sun $\sim 5800\text{ K}$). LED is $7.5\times$ more efficient than incandescent (6 W LED = 15 W CFL = 45 W bulb). Colour temp: 2700K warm to 6000K daylight. IR uses: fever thermometer, thermography, night vision, pit vipers, mosquitoes, Stinger missiles.

溫度越高峰值波長越短。鎢絲燈效率低於 5%，LED 比白熾燈節能 7.5 倍。紅外線應用於熱成像、夜視鏡、蝮蛇感熱及導彈追蹤。

Ultraviolet (UV) 紫外線

Photon energy $E = hf = hc/\lambda$. Sunlight UV is 95% UVA (315–400 nm, tanning/aging) & 5% UVB (280–315 nm, sunburn, skin cancer, cataracts, but produces Vitamin D). UVC (100–280 nm, short wave) destroys germs & purifies water. Other uses: black light fluorescence, forensics, banknotes, photolithography (chips), $\text{TiO}_2$ self-cleaning glass.

UVA 致黑老化；UVB 致曬傷、白內障但助合成維他命 D；UVC 波長最短能量最高，用於殺菌淨水。

Radio, Microwave, X-ray & Gamma 無線電波、微波、X射線與伽馬射線

Radio (3 kHz–300 GHz): AM (~100 kHz–30 MHz, long $\lambda$ reaches <300 km, lower sound quality/bitrate) vs FM (88–106 MHz, ~60 km). Microwaves (300 MHz–300 GHz): 2.45 GHz oven, 2.6 GHz 4G LTE, CW/pulse radar, radio astronomy (CMB, China's 500 m FAST). X-rays: fast electrons hit metal target (dental 0.005 mSv, chest 0.1 mSv, CT 6 mSv vs natural background 2.4 mSv/yr; airport backscatter). Gamma rays: emitted by radioactive nuclear decay.

AM 波長較長傳播更遠但音質較差；微波用於微波爐、雷達及 FAST 射電望遠鏡；X 光用於醫學造影（CT 約 6 mSv，自然背景約 2.4 mSv/年）；伽馬射線來自原子核衰變。

Chapter 2 · Force and Motion

Force & Motion 力與運動

What is Physics? Physics is an experimental science that explains how nature works (not philosophical why). If reliable, repeatable experimental results disagree with a proposed physical law, scientists must revise or replace the law (votes or mathematical elegance never override evidence). Brief History: Ancient Greeks (Aristotle, Archimedes) $\to$ 17th c. Mechanics (Galileo, Kepler, Newton) $\to$ 1800s EM & Thermodynamics (Faraday, Maxwell, Kelvin, Boltzmann) $\to$ Modern Physics (Röntgen X-rays 1895, Becquerel radioactivity 1896, Planck quantum 1900, Einstein relativity 1905, Bohr atom 1913).

物理是實驗科學，研究自然「如何」運作而非哲學上的「為何」。當可重複的實驗結果與定律矛盾時，必須修正或取代該定律。

Kinematics 運動學

$$v_{x,\text{avg}} = \frac{\Delta x}{\Delta t} = \frac{x_f - x_i}{\Delta t}, \qquad a_{x,\text{avg}} = \frac{\Delta v_x}{\Delta t}$$

On an $x$-$t$ graph, average velocity is the slope of the line connecting two endpoints. On a $v$-$t$ graph, average acceleration is the slope. A change in state of motion occurs only when acceleration $a \ne 0$ (constant velocity = no change in state of motion).

$x$-$t$ 圖的斜率為平均速度，$v$-$t$ 圖的斜率為平均加速度。只有當加速度不為零時，運動狀態才算改變。

Newton's Three Laws 牛頓三大定律

  • 1st Law (Inertia 慣性): If $\Sigma\vec{F} = 0$, an object stays at rest or moves at constant velocity (Galileo overturned Aristotle's view). In sudden braking, an unrestrained passenger moves forward relative to the car due to inertia (no forward force acts on them). 煞車時乘客前傾是因為慣性保持原本速度，並非受到向前的力。
  • 2nd Law ($\Sigma\vec{F} = m\vec{a}$): Always sum forces as vectors first to get net force. Airbags increase stopping time $\Delta t$, reducing deceleration $a$ and impact force $F$ on the head. 必須先求合外力；安全氣囊延長減速時間以減少衝擊力。
  • 3rd Law (Action-Reaction 作用力與反作用力): $\vec{F}_{A\text{ on }B} = -\vec{F}_{B\text{ on }A}$ — equal magnitude, opposite direction, acting on different bodies (never cancel each other on one body!). In a truck-car collision, both exert equal forces on each other (the lighter car has larger $a = F/m$). Applications: swimming, walking, skateboarding, jet engines, gun recoil, and rockets in vacuum ($F_{\text{thrust}} \approx \dot{m}v_e$; exhaust gas pushes rocket forward, no air needed). 作用力與反作用力大小相等、方向相反、作用於不同物體。貨車撞小車時受力相同；火箭在真空中靠向後噴氣獲得向前的反作用推力。

Mechanical Work & Energy 機械功與能量轉換

$$W = F d \cos\theta$$
  • $\theta < 90^\circ \Rightarrow$ Positive work (energy transferred TO object).
  • $\theta > 90^\circ \Rightarrow$ Negative work (energy removed FROM object).
  • $\theta = 90^\circ$ or $d = 0 \Rightarrow$ Zero work (e.g., upward force on horizontally moving box).
  • Holding a heavy box stationary: Zero mechanical work on the box ($d=0$), yet muscles consume chemical energy converted into heat, causing fatigue. 靜止捧著重箱子對箱子作功為零，但身體消耗化學能並轉化為熱能，所以會感到疲累。
  • Energy Conversion Pathways: Gasoline car (Chemical $\to$ Kinetic). EV from coal: Coal (Chemical) $\to$ Steam (Thermal/Kinetic) $\to$ Grid (Electrical, ~6–8% loss) $\to$ Li-ion Battery (Chemical) $\to$ Inverter/Motor (Mechanical Kinetic; regenerative braking recovers energy).

Normal Force & Friction 正向力與摩擦力

$$|f_s| \le f_{s,\max} = \mu_s N, \qquad f_k = \mu_k N \quad (\mu_k < \mu_s)$$

Normal force ($N$) is always perpendicular to the surface. Static friction ($f_s$) adjusts to match the applied force up to $f_{s,\max}$ (do NOT blindly set $f_s = \mu_s N$ when stationary!). Once sliding begins, friction drops to constant kinetic friction ($f_k$).

靜摩擦力會自動平衡外力直至最大值 $\mu_s N$；一旦滑動即降為較小的動摩擦力 $\mu_k N$。

Static friction drives motion: When walking or rolling a tire without slipping, your foot/wheel pushes ground backward, and ground exerts forward static friction on you.
Typical $(\mu_s, \mu_k)$: Rubber on dry concrete (1.0, 0.7), wet concrete (0.7, 0.5), shoes on wood (0.9, 0.7), shoes on ice (0.1, 0.05), Teflon on steel (0.04, 0.04), bone/synovial fluid (0.016, 0.015). 走路和汽車行駛時，地面給予的是「向前的靜摩擦力」（因為接觸面沒有打滑）。

Rotational & Circular Motion 圓周與轉動

Angular velocity: $\omega = \Delta\theta/\Delta t$ (independent of radius; minute hand $= 360^\circ/60\text{ min} = 6^\circ/\text{min}$). Linear speed $v = r\omega$. Tidal locking: Moon's rotation syncs with its orbit so the same side always faces Earth.

Centripetal force: $a_c = v^2/r$ inward. "Centrifugal force" is fictitious (it's inertia!). If a string breaks, the stone flies off along a straight tangent line. Cars skid on oil/ice when required $mv^2/r > f_{s,\max}$.

離心力是假想力（實為慣性）。繩子斷裂時石塊沿切線直線飛出；車速過快或路面結冰使靜摩擦力不足提供向心力時便會打滑。

Gravity & General Relativity 萬有引力與廣義相對論

Newton's Gravity: $F = \frac{Gm_1m_2}{r^2}$ (inverse square: doubling $r \Rightarrow F/4$). $G = 6.67430\times 10^{-11}\text{ m}^3\text{kg}^{-1}\text{s}^{-2}$ (measured by Cavendish torsion balance; least precisely known fundamental constant, relative uncertainty $2.2\times 10^{-5}$).

General Relativity: Mass curves spacetime; objects and massless light follow shortest paths (null geodesics), explaining gravitational lensing. Reduces to Newton when $v \ll c$, $r \gg r_s = 2GM/c^2$ (Schwarzschild radius), and fields are static. Black holes (Luminet 1979 sim; M87 EHT 2017) & Gravitational waves (LIGO GW150914 black hole merger).

距離加倍引力變為四分之一。廣義相對論指出質量彎曲時空，無靜止質量的光線也會沿彎曲時空偏折。LIGO 觀測到雙黑洞合併產生的引力波。

Seismic Waves & AM/FM 地震波與調幅/調頻

Fixed-end wave reflection inverts the pulse. Earthquake waves: P-waves (longitudinal, back-and-forth), S-waves (transverse, up-down/sideways), surface waves: Love waves (side-to-side) & Rayleigh waves (elliptical rolling).

Information Transmission: Encoding a message signal onto a carrier wave via AM (Amplitude Modulation) or FM (Frequency Modulation).

地震包含縱波 P 波、橫波 S 波，以及表面波（洛夫波與瑞利波）。無線電透過調幅（AM）或調頻（FM）將訊號編碼在載波上傳輸。

Chapter 3 · Thermal Physics #1 & #2

Thermal Physics 熱物理學

Temperature & 0th Law: Temperature $T$ is proportional to average molecular kinetic energy: $\frac{1}{2}k_B T$ per degree of freedom ($k_B = 1.38\times 10^{-23}\text{ J/K}$). Zeroth Law: If $A$ and $B$ are each in thermal equilibrium (no net energy transfer) with $C$, then $A$ and $B$ are in thermal equilibrium with each other ($T_A = T_B$).

溫度正比於分子平均動能（每個自由度 $\frac{1}{2}k_B T$）。第零定律：若 A、B 皆與 C 達熱平衡，則 A 與 B 亦熱平衡（同溫）。

$$C = K - 273.15, \quad C = \frac{F - 32}{1.8}, \quad F = 1.8K - 459.67, \quad C \approx 37 - \frac{100 - F}{2}$$

Absolute zero: $0\text{ K} = -273.15^\circ\text{C} \approx -460^\circ\text{F}$ (no thermal motion). Water freezes at $0^\circ\text{C} / 32^\circ\text{F} / 273\text{ K}$ and boils at $100^\circ\text{C} / 212^\circ\text{F} / 373\text{ K}$.

Thermal Expansion: Interatomic potential $V(r)$ is asymmetric (easier to move apart than closer), so heating increases average atomic spacing:

$$\frac{\delta L}{L} = \alpha\,\delta T, \qquad \frac{\delta A}{A} = 2\alpha\,\delta T, \qquad \frac{\delta V}{V} = 3\alpha\,\delta T = \beta\,\delta T$$

Significance: Rail buckling, thermometers, weathering, sea-level rise (1/3 to 1/2 from ocean thermal expansion), diesel tanker shrinkage in cold weather. Exceptions: (1) Negative thermal expansion in $\text{ZrW}_2\text{O}_8$ (0.3–1050 K); (2) Water anomaly ($0^\circ\text{C}$–$4^\circ\text{C}$): contracts when heated from 0 to 4°C, reaching max density at $4^\circ\text{C}$ so ice floats on top! Water Phase Diagram: Triple point at $0.01^\circ\text{C}, 0.006\text{ atm}$; Critical point at $374^\circ\text{C}, 217.7\text{ atm}$.

原子間位能曲線不對稱導致熱脹冷縮。例外：$\text{ZrW}_2\text{O}_8$ 呈負熱膨脹；水在 0–4°C 反常收縮，4°C 密度最大，因此冰浮於水面。

Heat ($Q$) is energy transferred due to $\Delta T$ ($Q>0$ absorbed by system; $Q<0$ lost). Units: $1\text{ cal} = 4.1868\text{ J}$, $1\text{ kcal} = 4186.8\text{ J}$ (average human needs ~2000 kcal/day; human mechanical efficiency ~25%), BTU (7000 BTU AC cools a bedroom). Heat Generation: Chemical (combustion), Nuclear (fission/fusion), Electrical (Joule heating), Frictional, Solar.

$$Q = c m \Delta T \quad (\text{No phase change}), \qquad Q = L m \quad (\text{Phase change})$$

Specific Heat ($c$): Water $4.186$, Air $1.005$, Iron $0.449$, Copper $0.38\text{ kJ/(kg}\cdot\text{K)}$. Latent Heat ($L$) breaks intermolecular bonds at constant $T$ (flat plateau on $T$-$Q$ curve): Water $L_F = 79.7\text{ kcal/kg}$, $L_V = 539\text{ kcal/kg}$.

無相變時用比熱 $Q=cm\Delta T$；相變時溫度不變（曲線平坦），熱量用於破壞分子鍵，用潛熱 $Q=Lm$。

3 Heat Transfer Modes & 1st Law 三種傳熱與熱力學第一定律

  • Conduction (傳導): Random molecular/electron motion; $P_{\text{cond}} = kA\frac{T_H - T_C}{L}$. High $k$: Silver (428), Copper (401), Al (235); Insulators: Air (0.026), Foam (0.024 $\text{W/m}\cdot\text{K}$).
  • Convection (對流): Bulk fluid motion driven by buoyancy (hot fluid expands, lower density, rises).
  • Radiation (輻射): Thermal EM waves; $P_{\text{rad}} = \sigma\epsilon A T^4$ (no medium required; Sun surface $5500^\circ\text{C}$, core $15\text{M }^\circ\text{C}$).
$$\text{1st Law: } dE = dQ - dW, \qquad W = \int_i^f p\,dV \text{ (Area under } p\text{-}V \text{ curve)}$$

Work is path-dependent (expansion $W>0$, compression $W<0$). Clockwise closed cycle on $p$-$V$ diagram yields positive net work $W_{\text{net}} > 0$. 氣體作功等於 $p$-$V$ 圖下的面積，順時針封閉循環輸出正淨功。

Carnot Cycle: Uses two reservoirs ($T_H, T_C$) with 2 isotherms ($\Delta U=0 \Rightarrow Q=W$) and 2 adiabats ($\Delta Q=0 \Rightarrow -W=\Delta U$). Expands at high $T_H$ (high $p$) and compresses at low $T_C$ (low $p$) to extract net work $W = Q_H - |Q_C|$.

$$\eta_{\text{Carnot}} = \frac{W}{Q_H} = 1 - \frac{|Q_C|}{Q_H} = 1 - \frac{T_C}{T_H} \quad (T \text{ in Kelvin!})$$

Entropy ($S$): Measure of disorder, randomness, or uncertainty about microscopic states. For reversible isothermal heat transfer, $\delta S = Q/T$. In a reversible Carnot cycle, $\Delta S_{\text{gas}} = \frac{Q_H}{T_H} + \frac{Q_C}{T_C} = 0$.

卡諾循環由兩條等溫線與兩條絕熱線組成，效率上限為 $1 - T_C/T_H$（必須用絕對溫度 K）。熵 $S$ 量度系統無序度與微觀不確定性。

Carnot Refrigerator (Reverse Cycle): Inputs work $W$ to pump heat $|Q_C|$ from cold to hot (components: Evaporator $\to$ Compressor $\to$ Condenser $\to$ Expansion Valve). Coefficient of Performance (COP):

$$K = \frac{|Q_C|}{|W|} = \frac{|Q_C|}{|Q_H| - |Q_C|} = \frac{T_C}{T_H - T_C}$$

Equivalent Statements of the 2nd Law: (1) No heat engine can beat Carnot efficiency between $T_H$ and $T_C$ (e.g., 75% engine between 0°C & 100°C is impossible since $\eta_C \approx 27\%$); (2) Cannot extract net work in a closed cycle from a single heat bath; (3) Heat does not spontaneously flow from cold to hot; (4) Total entropy of interacting systems cannot decrease ($\Delta S_{\text{total}} \ge 0$).

第二定律等價表述：沒有熱機效率能超過卡諾效率；無法從單一熱源在封閉循環中吸熱作功；總熵不會減少。

Chapter 4 · Electricity & Magnetism I & II

Electricity & Magnetism 電磁學

Coulomb's Law, Electric Field & Potential 庫侖定律、電場與電勢

$$F = \frac{1}{4\pi\epsilon_0}\frac{q_1 q_2}{r^2}, \quad \vec{E} = \frac{\vec{F}}{q_0}, \quad U(r) = \frac{1}{4\pi\epsilon_0}\frac{q_1 q_2}{r}, \quad V = \frac{U}{q}$$

Electron charge $e = -1.6\times 10^{-19}\text{ C}$; $\epsilon_0 = 8.85\times 10^{-12}\text{ C}^2/(\text{N}\cdot\text{m}^2)$. Electric force between two electrons is $4.17\times 10^{42}$ times stronger than gravity! Polarization & Induction: Comb attracts paper; neutral metal electroscope leaves spread either by contact transfer OR by induced charge polarization (no contact); storm cloud induces positive charge on hair.

Field Lines & Equipotentials: Field lines start at $+$, end at $-$. $V = 0$ does not necessarily imply $E = 0$. Equipotential surfaces are always $\perp \vec{E}$. Ink-jet printer deflects charged ink drops with uniform $\vec{E}$. Conductors in static equilibrium: $\vec{E} = 0$ inside, equipotential body $\Rightarrow$ Faraday Cage (car/airplane shields occupants from lightning). Lightning rod: sharp tip creates huge $\vec{E}$ (tip discharge) to safely conduct lightning to ground.

靜電平衡時導體內部電場為零（法拉第籠保護車內和機艙人員）；避雷針利用尖端放電將雷電導入大地。

Capacitors, Current & Ohm's Law 電容、電流與歐姆定律

$$q = C V, \qquad i = \frac{dq}{dt}, \qquad R = \frac{V}{i}, \qquad P = V I$$
  • Capacitors: Store electric energy on two opposite-charge plates ($+q, -q$); uniform $\vec{E}$ inside parallel plates. Used in photoflash, computer memory, and CCD (Charge-Coupled Device) light sensors (photodiode + capacitor "bucket-brigade" readout; cryo-cooled to cut noise).
  • Current & Cables: $1\text{ A} = 1\text{ C/s}$, flows in closed loops (same $i$ across varying wire cross-sections). Coaxial cables use an outer copper mesh tube as the electron return path.
  • Power & Safety: $220\text{ V} \times 1\text{ A} = 220\text{ W}$ (1 hr = $0.22\text{ kWh}$). Finger spark to doorknob is $40,000\text{–}100,000\text{ V}$ but harmless because current is tiny. HK 13A Plug: Live (brown, with melting Fuse for fire protection), Neutral (blue), Earth (green/yellow).
  • Materials: Conductors (Ag, Au, Cu, steel, seawater), Insulators (rubber, glass, oil, diamond, dry wood), Semiconductors (Silicon — microelectronics & solar photovoltaics).

CCD 結合光電二極管與電容逐行讀取電荷。香港三腳插頭設火線（連保險絲防過載起火）、中線與地線。矽是最常見的半導體材料。

Magnetic Field, Lorentz Force & Currents 磁場、勞侖茲力與電流磁效應

Magnets always exist as dipoles (N & S pairs; breaking a magnet creates two smaller dipoles; no magnetic monopoles found). Field lines emerge from N and enter S.

$$\vec{F}_B = q\vec{v}\times\vec{B}, \quad R = \frac{mv}{|q|B}, \quad B_{\text{wire}} = \frac{\mu_0 i}{2\pi r}, \quad F_{ba} = \frac{\mu_0 L i_a i_b}{2\pi d}$$
  • Right-Hand Rule: Sweep $\vec{v}$ into $\vec{B}$ $\to$ thumb gives $\vec{v}\times\vec{B}$ (reverse for negative $q$). $F_B = 0$ if $v=0$ or $\vec{v}\parallel\vec{B}$. Unit: Tesla ($1\text{ T} = 1\text{ N/(A}\cdot\text{m)}$; Earth $\sim 10^{-4}\text{ T}$, neutron star $10^8\text{ T}$).
  • Motion in $\vec{B}$: Since $\vec{F}_B \perp \vec{v}$, magnetic fields never do work on free charges! $\vec{v}\perp\vec{B} \Rightarrow$ circular motion; adding $v_\parallel \Rightarrow$ spiral helix.
  • Electric Motor & Solenoid: Current loop in $\vec{B}$ experiences torque; a commutator reverses current every half-turn for continuous rotation. Parallel currents attract; antiparallel currents repel. A solenoid generates a strong, uniform $\vec{B}$ inside.

Magnetic Materials, Earth & Induction 磁性材料、地磁與電磁感應

  • Origin of Magnetism: Electron spin & orbital angular momentum. Diamagnetism (weakly repelled by any pole, universal); Paramagnetism (weakly attracted); Ferromagnetism (Fe, Ni, Co — microscopic magnetic domains align irreversibly $\Rightarrow$ permanent magnets, hysteresis loop, hard disks).
  • Earth's Magnetic Field: 90% from liquid metal motion in outer core, 10% from atmospheric ions. Tilted $11.5^\circ$ from rotation axis; magnetic South pole is in the Northern Hemisphere. Recorded in Vatican mural hematite pigments (1740 vs today) & Mid-Atlantic Ridge seafloor (reverses every ~1M yrs). Van Allen belts trap solar electrons/protons in corkscrew paths $\to$ dipping into atmosphere creates Auroras.
  • Electromagnetic Induction: Magnetic flux $\Phi = BA\cos\theta$. Changing flux induces current (Faraday's Law). Lenz's Law: induced current produces a $\vec{B}$ field that opposes the change in flux. Rotating a coil in $\vec{B}$ generates sinusoidal AC voltage (electric generator).

地球磁場 90% 來自液態外核，地磁南極在北半球；帶電粒子沿地磁線螺旋運動撞擊大氣產生極光。磁通量變化產生感應電流（法拉第定律），其方向反抗磁通量變化（楞次定律），為發電機原理。

Chapter 5 · Quantum Physics 05 & 06

Quantum Physics 量子物理

Nature of Light & Photoelectric Effect 光之本質與光電效應

Historical debate: Democritus & Newton (Opticks 1704: particles) vs Descartes, Grimaldi (diffraction), Hooke, Young (1801 double-slit), Maxwell (1861 EM waves). Then Hertz (1887) & Lenard (1902) discovered the photoelectric effect: classical EM failed because emission depends on frequency $\nu$ (cutoff $\nu_c = \phi/h$), NOT intensity $I$! Intensity only controls the number of ejected electrons.

$$E_{\text{photon}} = h\nu = \frac{hc}{\lambda}, \qquad K_{\max} = eV_0 = h\nu - \phi \; (\nu \ge \nu_c)$$

Daily Applications: SPF 30 Sunscreen selectively blocks high-energy UV photons that break DNA bonds (why you don't look like a mirror or black in visible light); human vision (rhodopsin photopigment); solar panels, smoke detectors, CCD/CMOS sensors.

愛因斯坦提出光子假說：單個光電子能量僅取決於光頻率，光強只決定光電子數量。防曬霜專門吸收高能量紫外線以保護 DNA。

Compton Scattering & Matter Waves 康普頓散射與物質波

Compton Scattering: Photons collide with electrons like billiard balls, transferring energy & momentum so scattered wavelength increases ($\lambda' > \lambda$):

$$\lambda' - \lambda = \frac{h}{m_e c}(1 - \cos\phi), \qquad \lambda_{\text{de Broglie}} = \frac{h}{p} = \frac{h}{mv}$$

Single-Photon (Taylor 1909) & Electron Double-Slit: Particles arrive one-by-one as localized spots, yet accumulate into a wave interference pattern! Measuring "which slit" destroys interference. de Broglie (1924): All matter has wave properties (verified by 1927 electron crystal diffraction; at same speed, proton $\lambda_p = \lambda_e / 1836$). Fundamental links: $E = \hbar\omega$, $p = \hbar k$.

康普頓散射證實光子具有動量；單光子與電子雙縫實驗顯示微觀粒子同時具有波與粒子的性質（波粒二象性），德布羅意波長 $\lambda = h/(mv)$。

Wavefunction & Uncertainty 波函數與測不準原理

Probability density is $|\psi(x,t)|^2$; probability in $[a,b]$ is $\int_a^b |\psi|^2 dx$. Superposition $\psi = \psi_1 + \psi_2$ allows destructive cancellation ($|\psi_1+\psi_2|^2 = 0$ at dark fringes).

$$\Delta x\,\Delta p \ge \frac{\hbar}{2}, \quad \hbar = \frac{h}{2\pi}$$

Localized spike = tiny $\Delta x$, huge $\Delta p$. Plane wave = tiny $\Delta p$, huge $\Delta x$. Measurement irreversibly collapses $\psi$.

$|\psi|^2$ 給出找到粒子的機率密度；位置與動量無法同時精確測量，測量會使波函數坍縮。

Atomic Theory & Bohr Model 原子論與波耳模型

Dalton (1808 CO vs $\text{CO}_2$ simple ratios), Brownian motion (Brown 1827, Einstein 1905), Thomson (1897 electron), Rutherford (1911 nucleus — classical accelerating electrons would radiate & spiral in). Bohr (1913) quantized orbits via standing electron waves $2\pi r = n\lambda$:

$$E_{n,Z} = \frac{-13.6\text{ eV}\cdot Z^2}{n^2}, \quad h\nu = E_m - E_n$$

Explains discrete emission/absorption spectra: Lyman ($n=1$, UV), Balmer ($n=2$, visible), Paschen ($n=3$, IR).

波耳模型以駐波條件量子化電子能級，完美解釋氫原子離散光譜（萊曼、巴爾末、帕申系）。

Quantum Spin & Stern-Gerlach 量子自旋與史特恩-革拉赫實驗

Silver atoms in an inhomogeneous $\vec{B}$ split into 2 discrete spots ($S_z = \pm 1$), proving quantized spin. General state: $|\Psi\rangle = \alpha|\uparrow\rangle + \beta|\downarrow\rangle$ ($\alpha^2+\beta^2=1$; probabilities $\alpha^2, \beta^2$). $x$-basis: $|\rightarrow\rangle = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}(|\uparrow\rangle + |\downarrow\rangle)$, $|\leftarrow\rangle = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}(|\uparrow\rangle - |\downarrow\rangle)$.

Sequential SG: $Z\to Z$ gives 100% same; $Z\to X$ gives 50/50; $Z\to X\to Z$ gives 50/50 again (measuring $x$ resets $z$!). Schrödinger's Cat: $|\text{cat}\rangle = \alpha|\text{dead}\rangle + \beta|\text{alive}\rangle$.

銀原子束在非均勻磁場中分裂為兩束，證實自旋量子化。連續測量 $Z\to X\to Z$ 會再次出現 50/50 結果，展現量子態坍縮。

Chapter 6 · Atoms, Radioactivity, Nuclear & Particle Physics

Atoms, Nuclear & Particle Physics 原子、核物理與粒子物理

Scales & Models: Atom $\sim 10^{-10}\text{ m}$ ($0.1\text{ nm}$); Nucleus $\sim 10^{-15}\text{ m}$ (100,000$\times$ smaller); Quarks/Electrons $< 10^{-18}\text{ m}$. Models: Democritus (440 BC) $\to$ Dalton (1803 billiard ball) $\to$ Thomson (1904 plum pudding) $\to$ Rutherford (1911 tiny dense nucleus via 1909 Geiger-Marsden $\alpha$-gold foil scattering: 1 in 8000 deflected $\ge 90^\circ$) $\to$ Bohr (1913) $\to$ Schrödinger (1926 electron cloud).

Subatomic Particles & Isotopes: Proton ($+1e$, 1 amu, $1.67\times 10^{-27}\text{ kg}$), Neutron ($0$, 1 amu), Electron ($-1e$, $0.0005$ amu, $9.11\times 10^{-31}\text{ kg}$). Isotopes have same $Z$ (protons) but different neutrons: Carbon ($^{12}\text{C}, ^{13}\text{C}, ^{14}\text{C}$), Hydrogen (Protium $^1\text{H}$, Deuterium $^2\text{H}$ forming Heavy Water $\text{D}_2\text{O}$, radioactive Tritium $^3\text{H}$), Uranium ($^{234}\text{U}, ^{235}\text{U}, ^{238}\text{U}$).

同位素質子數相同、中子數不同。重水由氘（$^2\text{H}$）組成。原子核比原子小約十萬倍。

Radioactivity & Half-Life: Strong nuclear force is optimal at nuclear magic numbers; unstable nuclei emit ionizing radiation ($\alpha, \beta, \gamma$). Discovered by Becquerel (1896) & Pierre/Marie Curie (1898). Half-life ($T_{1/2}$): average time for half of nuclei to decay ($^{14}\text{C}: 5730\text{ yrs}$, dates organic fossils up to ~50,000 yrs).

Natural Radioactivity & Units: Human body has Potassium-40 (~4,300 Bq; also in KCl salt); air has Radon-222 (noble gas, #2 lung cancer cause, #1 in non-smokers). Units: $1\text{ Ci} = 3.7\times 10^{10}\text{ Bq}$; $1\text{ rad} = 0.01\text{ Gy}$; $1\text{ rem} = 0.01\text{ Sv}$; $\text{rem} = \text{rad}\times Q$ (Quality factor $Q$: X/$\gamma$/$\beta = 1$; $n/p = 10$; $\alpha = 20$). Limit: 20 mSv/yr avg (300 rem $\to$ 50% lethal without treatment). Shielding: $\alpha$ (paper), $\beta$ (Al sheet), $\gamma$/X (thick lead/iron), Neutrons (water/concrete).

碳-14 半衰期 5730 年用於古物定年；人體含鉀-40，空氣含氡氣（非吸煙者肺癌首因）。屏蔽能力：$\alpha$ 用紙、$\beta$ 用鋁片、$\gamma$/X 用鉛、中子用水或混凝土。

Nuclear Fission vs. Fusion 核裂變與核聚變

Energy comes from mass loss via $E = mc^2$ ($1\text{ kg } ^{235}\text{U} \approx 3,000\text{ tons coal} \approx 14,000\text{ barrels oil}$).

  • Fission (重核裂變): $n + {}^{235}_{92}\text{U} \to {}^{93}_{36}\text{Kr} + {}^{141}_{56}\text{Ba} + 2n + \text{energy}$ (chain reaction: 1 $n$ in $\to$ 2 $n$ out). Reactors slow neutrons with a moderator (usually water) for controlled steady-state flux ($\sim 10^{14}\text{ n}\cdot\text{cm}^{-2}\text{s}^{-1}$); weapons are uncontrolled.
  • Fusion (輕核聚變): D-T reaction ${}^2_1\text{H} + {}^3_1\text{H} \to {}^4_2\text{He} + {}^1_0n$ ($Q = 17.59\text{ MeV}$; ~14 MeV to neutron). Sun fuses 500 million tons H/s. Lawson's Criterion: high $T$, high density $n$, long confinement $\tau$ ($n\tau \ge 10^{20}\text{ s/m}^3$ for D-T).
  • Confinement & Blanket: Magnetic confinement (Tokamak: toroidal + poloidal $\to$ twisted helical field; JET, ITER) vs Inertial confinement (lasers hit 1 mm D-T pellet, $\tau \sim 10^{-11}\text{–}10^{-9}\text{ s}$). A ~1 m molten lithium blanket captures neutrons & breeds Tritium (${}^1_0n + {}^6_3\text{Li} \to {}^4_2\text{He} + {}^3_1\text{H}$). Pros: abundant fuel, no runaway meltdown, non-radioactive He product. Cons: limited helium for superconducting magnets, neutron structural damage/induced radioactivity.

核反應釋放的能量來自質量虧損（$E=mc^2$）。裂變需減速劑（水）控制連鎖反應；聚變需滿足勞森判據，透過托卡馬克磁約束或雷射慣性約束實現，並用液態鋰層吸收中子與增殖氚。
